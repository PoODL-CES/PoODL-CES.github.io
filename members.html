Sakhi Kundu

sakhikundu@iisc.ac.in

Evolution has always intrigued me by the way it has the power to shape the natural world. Questions like "Can we predict how a particular trait will change?" drive my research interests.

My research focuses on understanding how natural selection shapes genomic variation in wild populations. Using the Olive Ridley turtle (Lepidochelys olivacea) as a model system, I investigate mutation tolerance across genes, temporal and spatial variation in selection, and the impact of environmental change on genetic architecture.

Apart from academia, I value spending time with my loved ones, exploring wildlife, humming Rabindrasangeet, and just lazing around.

Research Interests: Evolutionary biology, population genetics, conservation.

Education: BSc in Life Sciences, Presidency University, Kolkata (2024)

Bramhi Chougule

chougulebramhi@gmail.com

Understanding the genetic makeup and population dynamics of endangered species is crucial for their conservation. My research focuses on the population genetics of the endangered Kondana Soft-furred Rat.

By studying population size, gene flow, genetic differentiation, genetic diversity, and inbreeding, I aim to gather insights that will aid in the protection and management of this species.

Research Interests: Population genetics, Conservation genetics, Phylogenetics, Ecology and Evolution.

Apoorva Sodhi

apoorva.sodhi@cwsindia.org | Twitter: @apoorva_sodhi

My interest in wildlife began with a fascination for animal behaviour and developed into a broader interest in ecology and conservation. With a background in Zoology, Wildlife Sciences and Biosciences, my research lies at the intersection of freshwater ecology, molecular ecology and conservation, with otters at the centre of much of my work.

I am currently a doctoral fellow at the Centre for Wildlife Studies and am collaborating with PoODL on research assessing otter population health across freshwater landscapes in India. Outside research, I enjoy photography, dance, music, travelling, hiking and exploring new places.
